The course introduces students to the deep interactions between global economics and international politics. The course focuses on historical roots of IPE, state cooperation, global/regional trade, and international finance. It also covers topics of globalization, state conflict, environmental issues, economic inequality, and global development. I assisted Nick Martini in running in-class discussions and activities that connected these topics to everyday examples and graded students' assignments.
