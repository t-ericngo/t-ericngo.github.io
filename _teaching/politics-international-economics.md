An introduction to the politics of the global economy – trade, money and finance, investment, and development. In my sections I connected abstract mechanisms to students' everyday lives: for instance, I handed out Vietnamese banknotes and asked students to look up the exchange rate and predict what a low-value currency means for the price of coffee imported from Vietnam in an Iowa store.
