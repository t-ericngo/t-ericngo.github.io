The politics of international trade and finance, from the rise of free trade in the 19th century and its breakdown between the world wars to the postwar trading system built around the World Trade Organization. The course also covers international monetary systems, relations between rich and poor countries, and global environmental politics. I ran discussion sections that connected these topics to everyday examples (such as using Vietnamese banknotes to show how exchange rates affect the price of imported coffee) and graded students' assignments.
