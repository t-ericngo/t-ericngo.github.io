An introduction to the politics of trade, money and finance, investment, and development. I ran the discussion sections, preparing slides, discussion materials, and activities that connected course concepts to everyday examples – such as using Vietnamese banknotes to show how exchange rates affect the price of imported coffee – and I guided, read, and graded students' assignments.
