An introduction to US foreign policy: its goals, themes, and patterns; the problems policymakers face; the means used in dealing with other nations and international organizations; and the processes and factors that shape how policy is made. I ran discussion sections built around group activities, presentations, and negotiation simulations, and guided and graded students' assignments, including a phased final paper.
