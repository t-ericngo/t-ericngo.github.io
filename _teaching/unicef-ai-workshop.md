An intensive, hands-on training program on the foundations, applications, and ethics of AI that I designed and delivered for more than 60 university-aged participants from diverse backgrounds. Participants trained a simple machine learning model to tell doodles of ducks from swans; rebuilt a simplified, code-free version of Google's AI flood-forecasting project to work through the life cycle of an AI application – gathering data, selecting variables, developing and evaluating a model, communicating risk, and implementing it; and, in a session on AI ethics, examined how generative AI can be misused for misinformation, surveillance, and manipulation. The aim was for participants to see AI as a set of legible tools with real benefits and risks, rather than as a mystery. I also advised UNICEF Vietnam on future directions for its AI training sessions and learning materials.
