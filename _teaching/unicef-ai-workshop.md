An intensive, hands-on program on the foundations, applications, and ethics of AI that I designed and delivered for more than 60 university-aged participants from diverse backgrounds. Through activities such as training a simple image classifier and rebuilding a code-free version of Google's AI flood-forecasting project, participants worked through the life cycle of an AI application, from data to deployment and risk. I also advised UNICEF Vietnam on future directions for its AI training sessions and learning materials.
