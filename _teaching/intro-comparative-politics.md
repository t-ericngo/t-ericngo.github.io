An introduction to political systems around the world, across all regions and levels of development, covering regime types, political change, political culture and public opinion, government structures, state–society relations, electoral systems, and public policy. I ran in-person (Fall 2022) and virtual (Spring 2023) sections built around discussion and group activities, and graded students' papers and assignments.
