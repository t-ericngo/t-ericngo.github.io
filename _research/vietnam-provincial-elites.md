title: "Local Elites and National Patrons: A LLM-Powered Database of Provincial Elites and Their Connections to Central Cabinet Elite Networks in Vietnam, 2000-2025"
status: ongoing
order: 3
authors:
  - Anh-Tu Ngo
  - My H. Nguyen
---

We are building an LLM-powered database of provincial elites in Vietnam (2000–2025) and their connections to central elite networks. These data allow us to examine how local elites connect to national patrons and shape government responses to foreign aid and investment.
