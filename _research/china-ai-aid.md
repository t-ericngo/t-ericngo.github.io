title: "How China Influences the Global South's AI Adoption Through Foreign Aid"

How does China use foreign aid to shape the adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) in the Global South? China has been one of the most active providers of development finance in the developing world, yet less is known about how such financial packages help recipient states to adopt AI as an emerging technology. We hypothesize that, like its other development finances, China's AI-related aid follow strategic considerations. Our two-stage selection models suggest that a state's AI aid reception depends on its technological capacity and diplomatic alignment. Once selection is accounted for, states facing terrorism and insurgency receive more, while those facing mass unrest – as well as those aligned with either the US or China – receive less. These patterns challenge the view that China simply exports AI to help governments suppress dissent.
